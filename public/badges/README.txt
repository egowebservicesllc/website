Put official partner badges here (PNG or SVG), then set their paths in
src/lib/site.ts → PARTNERS, e.g. googleCloudBadge: "/badges/google-cloud-partner.png".

Where to get them:
- Google Cloud Partner badge: Partner Advantage portal (partners.cloud.google.com) → marketing/brand assets
- AWS Partner badge: AWS Partner Central → Marketing → Badges (only once you are an AWS Partner)
- Microsoft partner badge: Microsoft Partner Center → Marketing (only once you are a Microsoft partner)

Use the files exactly as provided (no recolouring, cropping or redrawing).
